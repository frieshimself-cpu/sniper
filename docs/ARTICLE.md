# C.R.I.M.E. opens a case every hour. You pick who.

Chain Research & Investigation of Market Exploits. It has opened many cases. It has secured zero convictions. It is not a court.

## What it is

A filing clerk with a stamp. Once an hour it takes a name the crowd voted for, reads everything that name ever said on X, finds every wallet that name can be tied to, and replays what those wallets did on Solana while the posts were going up. Then it writes the case file.

The file has a headline, the findings, the receipts, the money trail and a narrative. Every receipt is a link: a post, a transaction, a wallet. If it cannot be linked, it is not in the file.

## How a case gets made

1. The poll. A human posts it on the X account: up to four handles, ten minutes. The public votes there.
2. The winner. The agent reads the result. It never writes to X. No votes, nothing opened.
3. Sweep. The winner's post history comes down. Every token mentioned, every "not selling", every address they ever typed.
4. Map. Wallets the handle posted themselves are theirs, claimed. Wallets a public label ties to them are probable. Anything weaker waits for a human.
5. Replay. The wallets' trades go next to the posts. Bought at 14:02. Posted "generational entry" at 14:09. Sold into you at 14:31.
6. File. Worst thing first. The file is on the site, every receipt a link.

## What counts as a finding

- A token they promoted that was pulled, dumped or bundled.
- A buy before the shill and a sell into the followers.
- "LP locked" while the LP was leaving.
- "Still holding" after the wallet emptied.
- A wallet they claimed that funded a bundle.
- A deleted post, when the chain remembers what it said.

Each one carries a score. Severity measures how much money moved and how clearly. Not guilt.

## The rules

Handles only. Never a legal name, a face, a city, an employer, a phone number. The agent reads public posts and public ledgers and stops there. If a source contains a personal detail, the source is dropped.

A wallet is shown next to a handle only with a link a reader can open. No source, no attribution.

Anyone who controls a wallet can dispute it by signing a message with it. The wallet disappears from every page at once. Not greyed. Gone. The reply is: Noted.

The agent reports what it found. It does not say what a court would say. One particular word never appears on the site.

Convictions: 0. Always. It is the joke and it is also true.

## Why

Every week someone with a hundred thousand followers calls a coin a generational entry and sells it nine minutes later. The posts stay up. The wallet is public. Nobody puts the two side by side. This does, on a schedule, with the receipts attached, for whoever you vote for.
